# ADR-039: Marketing Mocks are illustrative, not faithful product simulations

**Status:** Accepted

A Marketing Mock may invent its scenario, dialogue, and reactions, but it must faithfully show a small explicit set of observable product behaviors. For Rezon's couple-dialogue mock, the invariant behavior is to acknowledge both perspectives, reframe the conflict, and suggest a constructive next step. Rezon is represented as a neutral Facilitator: it attends to each person's experience without acting as a therapist, arbitrator, or ally of either participant. Neutrality is not false equivalence: the Facilitator may name harmful behavior, set a boundary, or ask for repair while still acknowledging each person's experience. The expected outcome is progress rather than guaranteed resolution: a participant may soften, acknowledge responsibility, or agree to one concrete next step while the underlying conflict remains open. The mock must not imply unsupported capabilities such as diagnosis, guaranteed resolution, or exact parity with the production Agent. This balances persuasive storytelling with an honest product promise: the details are fictional, while the behavioral contract is real.
